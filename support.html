Why does OnPace need my location?

OnPace uses GPS only during an active walk to measure your speed and guide your pace. Everything is processed on your device — nothing is uploaded.

Why “Always” and not only “While Using”?

A walk keeps running while your phone is in your pocket or the screen is locked. Outside an active walk OnPace does not record your location.

Why does OnPace need Motion & Fitness?

The step counter is a backup when the GPS signal is weak (for example between buildings). This keeps your pace and distance accurate in cities.

“Step sensor unavailable” — what do I do?

Go to iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Motion & Fitness → OnPace and make sure it is enabled.

Does OnPace work without an Apple Watch?

Yes. OnPace works fully on the iPhone alone. The Apple Watch adds hands-free haptic guidance and standalone tracking.

How do I add the Watch widgets?

Long-press the watch face → Edit → Complications → choose a slot → OnPace. For the Smart Stack: scroll up from the watch face, tap Edit, then +. There are two: “Start a walk” and “Last walk”.

How do I delete my walking history?

Open OnPace → History → swipe a walk to delete, or use the clear option. The history is also removed from your Apple Watch the next time the devices connect.

Does OnPace use my data for ads?

No. OnPace has no ads, no analytics and no data sharing. Everything stays on your device. See the Privacy Policy.
