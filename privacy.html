OnPace — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026
Short version: everything stays on your device. OnPace has no account, no server, no analytics and no ads.

What OnPace does NOT do

  • No account or login required
  • No data sent to any server
  • No tracking, analytics or advertising SDKs
  • No sale or sharing of personal information

What OnPace accesses (and why)

Location (GPS)

OnPace uses your location only during an active walk to measure your speed and distance and to guide your pace so you arrive on time. The “Always” permission is used so a walk keeps working while the phone is in your pocket or the screen is off.

  • Location is processed on-device only.
  • It is never uploaded, stored in the cloud, or shared.

Motion & Fitness (step counting)

The step counter is used as a backup when the GPS signal is weak (for example between buildings). Step data is processed on-device only.

HealthKit (Apple Watch only)

OnPace starts a workout session on Apple Watch so the haptic pace feedback keeps working while your wrist is down.

  • Health data stays in your Health app and is controlled by your iOS privacy settings.
  • OnPace does not write workouts to the Health app. The workout session is only used to keep the haptic feedback alive in the background.

Data storage

Walking history, saved routes and settings are stored locally on your device using Apple's standard storage. Your iPhone and Apple Watch exchange this data directly over Apple's WatchConnectivity — it never goes through a server. You can delete it at any time inside the app.

Third-party services

OnPace does not use third-party analytics, crash reporting or advertising networks. Apple Maps data is used only if you enable the optional live map, and it is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

Your rights

You can withdraw permissions at any time via:

  • iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → OnPace
  • iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Motion & Fitness → OnPace
  • iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → OnPace

Children

OnPace does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.
